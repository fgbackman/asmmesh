Rule: words of 4-9 letters, using the centre letter, each tile at most once.
"Also accepted" words are real but rarer: they score, but are never shown as missed.
Every word is listed, accurate or not to taste. Words the family filter would hide
(a game setting, on by default) are named again on their own line, so nothing is out of view.
`tools/ninefold/check_samples.py` checks this file independently.
